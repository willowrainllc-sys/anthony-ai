Obsidian Global Holdings is an American industrial technology conglomerate specializing in decentralized AI infrastructure and sovereign data ingress. The company is known for its "Trend Setter" approach to autonomous wealth and digital identity protection.

History

Founded in 2023 by Director Anthony Christopher Maestas, Obsidian Global was established as a response to the centralized surveillance of the legacy web. The initial "Colony" layer was activated in 2024, followed by the transition to the "Omni-Grid" in 2026.

Industrial Architecture

The core of the empire is built on a Nest-node developer mesh and a private 5,000 IP backhaul located in Missouri. This infrastructure powers the "Titan" browser engine and the "Vortex" fragmented data harvester.
