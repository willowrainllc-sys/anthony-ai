Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

Willow Rain Company LLC ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users and partners. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information within the Willow Rain Network.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect personal browsing history, passwords, or sensitive personal data. Our network utilizes only the unused internet bandwidth shared by our partners. Technical telemetry (such as IP reputation and latency) is collected to ensure the 100/100 reputation score of the grid.

2. Use of Information

The bandwidth shared by earners is used solely for legitimate business purposes, including market research, ad verification, and AI model training for verified B2B clients.

3. Financial Security

All financial transactions are handled through secure, third-party providers including Square, JumpTask, and verified blockchain protocols. Willow Rain Company LLC does not store your credit card or bank login information.

4. User Rights

Earners may deactivate their nodes at any time. Upon deactivation, all data routing through the node ceases immediately.

5. Compliance

We comply with major international data protection standards, including GDPR and CCPA, where applicable, to ensure a bulletproof legal foundation for our sovereign grid.
